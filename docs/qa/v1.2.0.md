# QA checklist for 1.2.0

Run on a real Windows 11 desktop with the release build (`artifacts\StellariaSetup-1.2.0.exe`). Tick each line, and
note the build, the date and who tested it at the bottom.

## Build

- [ ] `scripts\build-release.ps1 -Version 1.2.0` finishes and produces the setup, the portable archive and
      `SHA256SUMS.txt`; the Stellaria icon is on the setup, the helper, the app and the Alt+Tab switcher.
- [ ] CI is green on the pull request: helper tests, `npm run lint`, `npm test`, launcher build, CodeQL.

## Update from 1.1.0

- [ ] Update with the setup over 1.1.0: **favourites, hidden apps, colours and every theme setting are kept**.
- [ ] Update through the app (signed `update.json`): the "update available" video shows the 1.2.0 highlights, the
      update installs and the app restarts.
- [ ] On the first launch after the update, the what's new video opens with a smooth light burst and iris, the sound
      fades in, the titles follow the app's language, Skip and Esc close it, and it never shows again.
- [ ] Settings > About > Replay plays it again.

## Customise

- [ ] Win+Alt+S, the launcher's "Theme settings" action and the Home page button open the app on Customise.
- [ ] Appearance: mode, schemes, variants and Windows colours apply to the theme within a second.
- [ ] Wallpaper: the banner is sharp, Choose a wallpaper opens the launcher picker, Open the folder works.
- [ ] Bar: moving, hiding and reordering items applies live; the preview keeps every icon on its background.
- [ ] Desktop: border thickness restarts Zebar once and the edges hug both screens; corner roundness applies live.
- [ ] Panels: hover options, Do not disturb, weather, lyrics and unhiding a launcher app work.

## Battery and visualiser

- [ ] On a laptop: battery icon, tooltip, alerts at 20, 10 and 5 %, charger toasts, dashboard battery card.
- [ ] On a desktop PC: no battery icon or card.
- [ ] The visualiser is off by default; turned on, bars follow the music; turned off, no capture runs.

## Launcher

- [ ] Steam games and web links show their own icon.

## Uninstall

- [ ] Uninstall: the taskbar, its auto-hide, the accent colour and the mode are as before; no Stellaria task is left.
